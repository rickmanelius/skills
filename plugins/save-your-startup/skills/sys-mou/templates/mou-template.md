# MOU Template

Adapted from Michael Girdley's sample MOU (X, April 21, 2023, https://x.com/girdley/status/1649393986899959813). His original is transcribed in `../references/girdley-mou-sample.md`.

**How to use it.** Everything above the line below is guidance. Copy only what is below the line into the draft, replace every bracket, and delete any row or bullet that does not apply rather than leaving it empty. For three or more partners, add a column to each table and a block under "Who owns what".

**Budget.** 400 to 600 words in total, 750 at most. Page 1 holds Vision through Structure; page 2 holds First rocks, Open questions and What this is. Mike's page 1 is 365 words and fills the page.

| Section | Budget |
|---|---|
| Vision | 1 or 2 sentences |
| How we will run it | up to 3 bullets |
| Who puts in what | one table |
| Who owns what | 3 to 5 bullets per person |
| Structure | up to 5 bullets |
| First rocks | 3 to 5 rows |
| Open questions | as many as are real; zero is fine |
| What this is | 2 lines plus the agreed line |

---

# Memorandum of Understanding: [Venture name]

[Partner A full name] and [Partner B full name]. Draft v[N], [YYYY-MM-DD].

## Vision

[What we are building, for whom, and how big it could get. A stranger should be able to repeat it after one read.]

## How we will run it

- [What we optimize for. Example: "Reinvest all profit for the first three years."]
- [How involved the owners are, and when that changes.]
- [How much risk we take at once. Example: "One location at a time until one is profitable for six straight months."]

## Who owns what

- [Verb-first outcome or decision they own. Example: "Hires the general manager."]

**[Partner B]**
- [Verb-first outcome or decision they own.]

## Structure

- **Entity:** [Type, or "None yet. [Name] confirms the type with a CPA by [date]."]
- **Ownership:** [A]% [Partner A], [B]% [Partner B], because [one reason]. [Vesting terms, if any.]
- **Decisions:** [Who decides day to day; what needs both of us; how we break a tie.]
- **Money out:** [Salaries, distributions, or reinvest, and until when.]
- **Exit:** [Buy-sell terms with a clock, e.g. 30 days to respond and 90 to close; what happens if someone stops putting in their time.]

This memo records what we agree on in principle. It is not a contract. We will turn it into formal agreements with a lawyer by [date or trigger]. Until then, when we remember things differently, we start from what this page says.
